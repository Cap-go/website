**Self-serve plans run on our standard [Terms of Service](https://capgo.app/tos/) and DPA.** Custom paperwork is included in the Enterprise plan (from US$15,000/year). This page lists exactly what is and is not available, so your procurement team can decide fast.

</Aside>

## Why we work this way

Capgo is a small, lean team. Self-serve plans cost a few hundred to a few thousand dollars per year. A single custom agreement review costs more than that in legal time, so we only do it where the contract size covers it.

Most procurement teams accept self-serve plans on standard terms once they see the architecture:

- **End-to-end encryption**: bundles are encrypted with your keys. Capgo never sees your code.
- **Your domain**: updates are served from your own domain, not ours.
- **Open source**: the full stack is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/Cap-go/capgo). You can self-host at any time, so no source escrow is needed.
- **No PII required**: Capgo only needs anonymous device identifiers to deliver updates.

## Legal and contracts

| Request | Self-serve | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Terms of Service and DPA | Included | Included |
| Capgo NDA template | Included | Included |
| Custom MSA or redlines of our terms | No | Included |
| Customer DPA, SCCs, sub-processor review | No | Included |
| Custom SLA with service credits | No | Included |
| Customer NDA template | No | Included |
| Non-standard governing law | No | Case by case (Estonia / EU default) |
| Source code escrow | No (open source) | No (open source) |
| Insurance certificates (cyber, E&O, general liability) | No | No |

Capgo does not carry cyber or errors-and-omissions insurance. Our customers' exposure is covered by the architecture above, not by a policy.
