<Aside type="note">

**All plans run on our standard [Terms of Service](https://capgo.app/tos/) and DPA by default.** Custom paperwork is a **Procurement add-on: from US$5,000 per year, available on any plan, included in Enterprise.** This page lists exactly what is and is not available, so your procurement team can decide fast.

</Aside>

## Why we work this way

Capgo is a small, lean team. Self-serve plans cost a few hundred to a few thousand dollars per year. A single custom agreement review, plus the yearly questionnaires and renewals that follow, costs more than that in legal and admin time. The add-on starts at US$5,000 per year and scales with the amount of paperwork, so it covers that time and nothing more. It is annual because procurement work repeats every year.

Most procurement teams accept self-serve plans on standard terms once they see the architecture:

- **End-to-end encryption**: bundles are encrypted with your keys. Capgo never sees your code.
- **Your domain**: updates are served from your own domain, not ours.
- **Open source**: the full stack is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/Cap-go/capgo). You can self-host at any time, so no source escrow is needed.
- **No PII required**: Capgo only needs anonymous device identifiers to deliver updates.

## Getting started

If your company needs anything in the add-on column, [contact us](https://capgo.app/contact/) with the list of documents your procurement team requires. We add the Procurement add-on to your plan and reply with a timeline. Enterprise customers already have it.

If none of those apply, sign up on any plan and start deploying today. Standard terms are accepted in the console.

## What the add-on never includes

- Insurance certificates of any kind.
- Source code escrow. The code is already public.
- Staff background checks.
- Phone or on-site support.
- Liability above the fees paid in the last twelve months.

These are not negotiable at any price. They are the reason we can keep the add-on cheap.
